---
type: mobilization
id: mob-piriformis-figure-four
title: Supine figure-four piriformis stretch
targets: [FMA:19082]
joint: FMA:35178
kind: stretch
steps:
  - Lie on your back with both knees bent and feet flat.
  - Cross the ankle of the affected side over the opposite knee.
  - Reach through and draw the uncrossed thigh toward the chest until a stretch is felt deep in the buttock.
  - Keep the pelvis level on the floor and the crossed knee relaxed outward.
duration: 30 to 45 seconds, 3 repetitions
frequency: Daily; after prolonged sitting
contraindications:
  - Hip replacement with posterior approach (check hip precautions first)
  - Acute lumbar disc symptoms with leg pain that worsens in flexion
  - Numbness or tingling in the leg during the stretch
sources: [travell-simons-vol2, neumann-kinesiology]
---
The figure-four position combines hip flexion with external rotation and abduction,
lengthening piriformis. Above about 60 degrees of hip flexion piriformis becomes an
internal rotator, so keeping the thigh nearer 90 degrees and adding adduction gives a
stronger stretch on the muscle.
