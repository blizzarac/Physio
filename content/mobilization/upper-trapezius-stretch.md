---
type: mobilization
id: mob-upper-trapezius-stretch
title: Seated upper trapezius stretch
targets: [FMA:9626]
kind: stretch
steps:
  - Sit tall and anchor the hand of the side to be stretched under the chair seat.
  - Let the opposite ear drop toward the shoulder until a gentle pull is felt on the anchored side.
  - Breathe slowly; do not pull the head with the free hand beyond a mild stretch.
  - Return to neutral and repeat on the other side.
duration: 30 seconds per side, 3 repetitions
frequency: 2 to 3 times daily during desk work
contraindications:
  - Acute neck injury or recent whiplash
  - Cervical instability or known vertebral artery insufficiency
  - Dizziness, visual disturbance, or arm symptoms during the stretch
sources: [travell-simons-vol1]
---
A low-load, long-duration stretch of the upper fibres of trapezius, useful after
sustained shoulder elevation. Anchoring the hand depresses the shoulder girdle so
the stretch is felt in the muscle rather than pulling on the neck.
