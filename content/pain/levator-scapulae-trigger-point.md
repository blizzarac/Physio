title: Levator scapulae trigger point ("stiff neck")
structure: FMA:32519
kind: trigger_point
referral_regions: [FMA:9626, FMA:13379]
common_causes:
  - Turning the head to one side for long periods (screen placed off-centre)
  - Sleeping with the neck laterally flexed
  - Walking with a cane that is too long
aggravating:
  - Rotating the head toward the painful side
  - Reaching across the body
relieving:
  - Elbow support to unload the shoulder girdle
  - Sustained gentle stretch with the arm anchored behind the back
red_flags:
  - Neck stiffness with fever or headache
  - Sudden severe neck pain after trauma
  - Numbness or weakness in the arm
sources: [travell-simons-vol1]
---
Levator scapulae trigger points produce pain at the angle of the neck and along the
medial border of the scapula, and are the most frequent muscular cause of a "stiff
neck" with limited rotation. Patients characteristically turn the whole trunk to
look behind them.
